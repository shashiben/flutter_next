## 1.0.0

- Compose fade, move, scale, rotate, skew, size, blur, tint, radius, reveal, and shadow with `NextAnimated`.
- The default easing is `NextSpring.smooth`. `gentle`, `snappy`, and `bouncy` are the other springs.
- One `NextMotion` spec and one `NextMotionView` player.
- Curves, delays, and reduced motion are part of playback.
- `playWhenVisible` starts the motion when the widget enters the viewport.
